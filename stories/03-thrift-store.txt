I lost 8 hours in a thrift store basement and I still can't explain it
A few years ago, My wife and I went to a thrift store on the side of the highway. We were traveling back home from the holidays and making a little road trip out of it. The time was exactly 7:00 am when we rolled in to the parking lot of the thrift store (I know this because my son was 8 months old at the time and we set alarms for his bottle feedings). We agreed on only spending 30 ish minutes in the thrift store, as we had to get back on the road soon. We went in, shopped around, but we got separated at one point. We were the ONLY ones in the store. I went through a door in the back that had a sign that read “more antiques downstairs”, this intrigued me and my trinket obsession, so I made my way downstairs. I was down there for maybe 20 minutes!? They had a LOT of vintage clothing and I was HYPED! 😂 I filled up my little basket with a shirt and some old children’s book. There was a kitchen down there also and a bathroom with the door that’s been ripped off. I just remember feeling super peaceful down there, it was quiet and I could hear the birds + highway car sounds. I was completely aware and awake the entire time. About 20 minutes pass and I made my way back up the stairs as I had felt it had been a few minutes too long and my wife would be looking for me. I was greeted by almost 10 police men, my crying wife, a screaming baby, and everyone was asking me where I was. I was SO confused. It took me a whole 5 minutes to realize the police had been called for ME- my wife thought I was missing. I remember being like “what!? What are you talking about?! I was gone for 20 minutes maybe!”. I will never forget the look on her face. She told me it was almost 3 in the afternoon. After talking with the police they went and looked downstairs for mold or anything that could have caused me to have brain fog and/or pass out. Like literally anything that would explain the time jump. Everyone was baffled. It was a few years ago, but every time I think about it I feel sick. 8 hours had passed and it felt like 20 minutes. I don’t have an explanation. Weird as hell!
