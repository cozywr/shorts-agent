Hot take - Kurt Cobain didn't shoot up right before he died, in fact, he died in withdrawal...

One thing to know about the levels of "heroin" in his system, is that the test didn't actually measure heroin - it measured a metabolite (breakdown product) of heroin...

Death can make interpreting these types of metabolite readings very difficult, because metabolism slows and then stops at death...

We know Kurt had a very high tolerance for heroin

We don't know whether the amount found in his blood stream represents a high or low amount for Kurt

We do know that Courtney had shut off his debit cards days prior to his death

We know he jumped the wall at rehab, a place where he would not have had access to his usual amounts of heroin (they may have had him on some controlled meds to ease withdrawal)

We know that his usual dealers were all trying to dodge him during this time because they didn't want to be responsible for a Rockstar's death

We know he called Mark Lanagan, one of his heroin connections, the day that he killed himself

We know that he died with a burning, nauseus, stomach

We know that the heroin rig found in the greenhouse was empty, neat, and possibly unused

I propose to you that, in addition to everything else that led Kurt towards suicide (depression, stress) - the final straw was withdrawal

If Kurt had been able to get one more hit, he may not have ended himself in that moment

He would have really benefitted from a harm reduction program rather than abstinence approach...
